Privacy Policy

At PharmaHub, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the highest level of security for your medical records and personal data.

Information Collection & Use

We collect essential information to process your healthcare orders, including your name, contact details, and delivery address. This data is collected during account registration and the checkout process to ensure a seamless connection between customers and local pharmacies.

Prescription & Health Data

As a dedicated healthcare platform, we treat your prescriptions with maximum confidentiality. Uploaded documents are encrypted and only accessible to authorized licensed pharmacists for order verification purposes.

Data Sharing Disclosure

PharmaHub does not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties. We only share necessary delivery details with our partner pharmacies to facilitate your medicine fulfillment.

Data Security Protocols

We implement industry-standard security measures, including SSL encryption and secure database management, to protect your sensitive information from unauthorized access or disclosure.
